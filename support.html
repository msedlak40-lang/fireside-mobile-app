Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a Fire group?

Go to the Fire tab, tap "+ Create Fire", enter a name and description, then share the invite code with your brothers.

How do I start an Interactive Journey?

Go to Study → Interactive Journeys, choose a journey, tap "Start Journey". You'll progress through locations, stories, and character profiles.

How do I save devotion highlights?

While reading a devotion, long-press any paragraph, select the text you want, and tap "Save Highlight".

What are Battle Verses?

Battle Verses are scriptures you save with themes (like fear, anger, temptation) to help you in spiritual warfare. Long-press any verse to save it.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at support@firesidefellowship.app with "Account Deletion" in the subject, and we'll process your request within 48 hours.

Is my data secure?

Yes! All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell your information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I use Fireside Fellowship on multiple devices?

Yes! Your data syncs across all devices where you're logged in with the same account.

How do I report inappropriate content in a Fire group?

Email us immediately at support@firesidefellowship.app with details. We take this seriously and will investigate promptly.
